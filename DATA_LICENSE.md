# Data attribution and license

Source: Chen, D. (2012). *Online Retail II* [Dataset]. UCI Machine Learning Repository. https://doi.org/10.24432/C5CG6D

Official record: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/502/online+retail+ii

The source dataset is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. The original workbook is redistributed without modification. Derived transaction, feature and prediction tables are adaptations; the study performs overlap reconciliation, exclusions, customer/date aggregation and model predictions. Attribution to the source is retained. No personal account credentials, private research correspondence or screenshots are included. The customer identifiers are the numeric identifiers supplied by the public source dataset.
